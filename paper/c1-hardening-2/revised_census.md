## 3. Plain Summary of How the User's Two Groups of Papers Are Affected

1. **The User's Two 2023 Camp 1 Papers (`Barak et al. 2023`, id 8; `Gupta et al. 2023a`, id 9)**:
   - **Direct Thermodynamic Contradiction**: Both papers use the Camp 1 constitutive pairing — placing the thermal stress $-\beta_{ij}T$ inside the nonlocal differential relation $(1 - \ell^2\nabla^2)t_{ij} = c_{ijkl}e_{kl} - \beta_{ij}T$ while leaving the dilational heating rate $T_0\beta_{ij}\dot{u}_{i,j}$ in the energy equation purely local. By **Theorem 1**, this pairing violates Maxwell's Fréchet-derivative symmetry in both Helmholtz and Gibbs representations and violates the global Second Law on closed thermo-mechanical cycles.
   - **Direct Boundary-Condition Error**: Both papers also impose interface traction continuity at $x_3 = 0$ on the auxiliary **local** stress $\sigma_{33}, \sigma_{31}$ instead of the physical Cauchy (nonlocal) stress $t_{33}, t_{31}$ that enters the momentum balance $t_{ij,j} = \rho\ddot{u}_i$, and omit Constitutive Boundary Conditions.
2. **The User's Eleven 2022–2026 Camp 2 Papers (`ids 15–25`)**:
   - **Bulk Operator Cancellation ($\gamma = 0$)**: All 11 papers apply the identical single-phase ($\gamma = 0$) nonlocal operator — either $(1 - \ell^2\nabla^2)$ in `ids 15–21` or $(1 - \xi^2\nabla^2 + \tau^2\partial_t^2)$ in `ids 22–25` — to the stress, the entropy density, and the heat flux vector. Because the energy balance $\rho T_0\dot{\eta} = -q_{i,i}$ contains only $\eta$ and $q_i$, the nonlocal operator factors out of the entire energy equation and is cancelled in every one of these 11 papers. Consequently, the bulk heat equation solved in all 11 papers is **identically the local heat equation**, and all reported nonlocal effects come solely from the momentum equation and boundary stress symbol.
   - **Singular $\gamma = 0$ Boundary Closure on Half-Spaces**: Three of these papers (`Yadav et al. 2022`, `Das et al. 2024`, `Das et al. 2025`) explicitly start from Eringen's integral convolution over the half-space $z \ge 0$ and pass to the differential operator $(1 - \ell^2\nabla^2)$ without enforcing the Robin CBCs $(\partial_z - \ell^{-1})w(0) = 0$ (which, for the single-phase $\gamma = 0$ kernel, have no solution for generic boundary tractions — the Romano et al. 2017 / Pham & Vu 2024 half-space paradox). The remaining 8 papers start directly from the differential Form A relation on $z \ge 0$ and invert it mode-by-mode in the traction BCs at $z = 0$.
   - **How C1 Relates to Them**: Publishing C1 openly documents that (a) the user's two 2023 Camp 1 papers used a thermodynamically inconsistent stress–entropy pairing, and (b) the user's eleven 2022–2026 Camp 2 papers operated in the $\gamma = 0$ all-field cancellation limit where the nonlocal operator drops out of the bulk heat PDE and where integral-to-differential equivalence on a half-space lacks CBC closure unless regularized to $\gamma \in (0, 1)$ (or reinterpreted strictly as a Polizzotto-type higher-order stress-gradient model with modified Gibbs entropy and higher-order boundary conditions).
