# `paper/c2-hardening/` — Candidate C2 Hardening & Falsification Audit (with C2+C3 Pivot Blueprint)

This directory contains the complete hostile prior-art audit, step-by-step symbolic/numerical derivation audit, counterexample search, physical parameter grounding, venue assessment, and next-step blueprints for **Candidate C2** (and its recommended **C2+C3 synthesis pivot**) from `paper/drive-analysis/candidates.md`.

## Files in This Directory

- `c2_hardening_report.md` — Master executive and technical summary covering Step 0 (environment verification) and Tasks 1–7.
- `task1_prior_art_audit.md` — **Task 1**: Hostile prior-art audit comparing C2 and C3 equation-by-equation against 15 primary papers from the Google Drive corpus and external literature.
- `task2_derivation_audit.md` — **Task 2**: Step-by-step derivation and edge-case audit (Theorem 2.1 frequency-domain passivity and fading-memory step-function obstruction; Theorem 2.2 corrected 5-field Lumer–Phillips contraction semigroup; Theorem 2.3 5-branch high-frequency Puiseux asymptotics; Section 5 audit of all 6 degenerate limits).
- `task3_counterexamples_falsification.md` — **Task 3**: Four explicit mathematical counterexamples and failure modes (bounded-domain Poincaré stabilization when $k + k^*(\tau_v - \tau_q) < 0$ and $\tau_T > 0$; unfiltered mechanical multiplier failure; norm degeneration at $K^*_{ij}\to 0$; bimaterial $\tau_q^- \ne \tau_q^+$ interfacial multiplier obstruction).
- `task4_physical_grounding.md` — **Task 4**: Quantitative grounding on four real physical media (Cu at 293 K, Si at 300 K, cryogenic NaF at 15 K, biological tissue at 310 K) and passivity audit of six published TPL parameter sets.
- `task5_scope_venue_realism.md` — **Task 5**: Realistic referee and venue assessment across *Math. Mech. Solids*, *Int. J. Heat Mass Transfer*, *J. Therm. Stresses*, *Wave Motion*, *ZAMP*, and *Mech. Res. Commun.*
- `task6_7_decision_and_blueprint.md` — **Tasks 6 & 7**: Explicit **PIVOT** decision and complete, ready-to-write blueprints for **Blueprint A (Primary Recommended Pivot: C2+C3 Imperfect-Interface Synthesis Note)** and **Blueprint B (Narrowed-Scope Bulk Fallback: C2 Poincaré-Cutoff & Wavefront Audit Note)**.
- `verify_c2_hardening.py` — Self-contained SymPy + NumPy symbolic and numerical verification script.
- `test_c2_hardening.py` — Pytest test suite (6/6 passing).
- `verification_results.json` — Machine-verified symbolic and numerical outputs generated by `verify_c2_hardening.py`.
- `c2-hardening.zip` — Distributable archive containing all files in `paper/c2-hardening/`.

## Reproducing the Symbolic & Numerical Audit

```bash
/tmp/venv/bin/python3 paper/c2-hardening/verify_c2_hardening.py
/tmp/venv/bin/pytest paper/c2-hardening/test_c2_hardening.py -v
```
