# Task 1: Hostile Prior-Art Audit for Candidate C2 (and Companion Audit of C3)

## 1. Executive Summary of the Prior-Art Audit

Candidate **C2** (*"Thermodynamic Passivity and High-Frequency Wavefront Classification of the Unified Three-Lag Green–Naghdi Thermoelastic Template"*, from `paper/drive-analysis/candidates.md`) proposes three core results for the first-order-in-$\tau_q$ three-phase-lag (TPL) / Green–Naghdi Type-III heat-flux law (`review paper.pdf`, Eq. 14) coupled to linear anisotropic thermoelasticity:

1. **Claim C2-1 (Frequency-Domain Passivity & Time-Domain Dissipation Inequality)**: Necessary and sufficient conditions on $(\tau_q, \tau_T, \tau_v, K_{ij}, K^*_{ij})$ for $\operatorname{Re} G_{ij}(-\mathrm{i}\omega) \succeq 0$ for all $\omega \in \mathbb{R}\setminus\{0\}$, namely
   $$K_{ij} + (\tau_v - \tau_q)K^*_{ij} \succeq 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad \tau_T \tau_q K_{ij} \succeq 0,$$
   together with an explicit quadratic Lyapunov functional $\mathcal{E}(t)$ satisfying $\dot{\mathcal{E}}(t) \le 0$.
2. **Claim C2-2 (3D Coupled Thermoelastic Well-Posedness & Contraction Semigroup)**: Lumer–Phillips generation of a contraction $C_0$-semigroup on a 5-component product Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$, plus Routh–Hurwitz exponential instability when $K_{ij} + (\tau_v - \tau_q)K^*_{ij} \prec 0$.
3. **Claim C2-3 (High-Frequency Principal-Symbol & Wavefront Speed Classification)**: Demonstration that $\tau_T > 0, \tau_q > 0$ yields a **parabolic** thermal branch with infinite propagation speed ($\lambda_{\text{diff}}(\xi) \sim -\frac{k\tau_T}{\rho c_E \tau_q}\xi^2$) alongside a finite-speed elastic wave $c_1$, whereas the strictly **hyperbolic** two-wavefront regime ($v_\pm < \infty$) occurs if and only if $\tau_T = 0, \tau_q > 0$ (the Moore–Gibson–Thompson / Lord–Shulman limit).

## 4. What Is Genuinely Dead vs. What Remains Open

1. **Dead (Already Published)**:
   - Bulk passivity inequalities $K_{ij} + (\tau_v - \tau_q)K^*_{ij} \succeq 0$ and $\tau_T\tau_q K_{ij} \succeq 0$ for the $(1,1,1)$ TPL model, and $K_{ij} + (\tau_v - \tau_q)K^*_{ij} \succeq 0$, $\tau_T K_{ij} - \frac{\tau_q}{2}(K_{ij} + \tau_v K^*_{ij}) \succeq 0$ for the $(2,1,1)$ TPL model.
   - The 3D time-domain Lyapunov functional and Lumer–Phillips contraction semigroup proof for bulk MGT/TPL thermoelasticity using the filtered displacement $\hat{u}_i = u_i + \tau_q\dot{u}_i$.
   - The bulk high-frequency classification ($\tau_T > 0 \implies$ parabolic infinite thermal speed; $\tau_T = 0 \implies$ hyperbolic MGT finite speeds).
2. **Open & Genuinely Novel (Survives Hostile Audit)**:
   - **Direction A (Primary Pivot — C2+C3 Synthesis on Imperfect Interfaces)**: *Passivity-Consistent Kapitza–Spring Imperfect Interfaces in Three-Phase-Lag and MGT Thermoelasticity: Interfacial Lyapunov Stability, the $\tau_q^- \ne \tau_q^+$ Multiplier Obstruction, and Exact Scattering Unitarity.*
   - **Direction B (Narrowed Bulk Fallback — Bounded-Domain Poincaré Stabilization vs. Constitutive Passivity)**: Even in bulk TPL thermoelasticity, our Task 2/3 mathematical audit uncovered a sharp discrepancy between **whole-space constitutive passivity** ($k + k^*(\tau_v - \tau_q) \ge 0$) and **bounded-domain Routh–Hurwitz modal stability** when $\tau_T > 0$: because of the fourth-order damping term $k\tau_T\tau_q|\nabla\dot{\theta}|^2$, the exact Routh–Hurwitz stability threshold on a bounded domain $\Omega$ with first Dirichlet eigenvalue $\mu_1(\Omega) > 0$ is
     $$k + k^*(\tau_v - \tau_q) + \frac{k\tau_T(k + k^*\tau_v)}{\rho c_E}\mu_1(\Omega) > 0 \qquad (\text{uncoupled})$$
     (and its exact $\varepsilon$-coupled counterpart derived in Task 2), proving that bounded domains with $\mu_1(\Omega) > \xi_{\text{crit}}^2$ are **exponentially stable even when the Quintanilla–Racke (2008) / Chiriţă et al. (2016) constitutive passivity condition $k + k^*(\tau_v - \tau_q) \ge 0$ is strictly violated**.
