# Literature and novelty assessment

Targeted investigation, not exhaustive. No priority claim is justified. Source details and verification boundaries are in `REFERENCE_VERIFICATION_REPORT.md`.

| Primary work | Problem/result and method inspected | Relation and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Cannarsa–Martinez–Vancostenoble (2020), arXiv Theorem 2.1 | Positive-exponential biorthogonals with explicit gap-dependent upper bounds, using complex-analytic constructions. | Exact input for fast interpolation; does not establish the coupled-tail kernel. Theorem and bound checked; not an independent verification of its proof. |
| Almira (2007), Theorem 29 | Full Müntz uniform density on intervals away from zero. | Gives fast-only annihilators when sum reciprocal rates converges; does not address the perturbed coupled rows. |
| Fattorini–Russell (1971) | Parabolic controllability and exponential moment method; historical role confirmed by CMV introduction. | Publisher metadata inspected; original full text was paywalled, so detailed theorem hypotheses and proof not audited. |
| Guerrero–Imanuvilov (2013), original PDF Theorem 1.1 and §2 | Boundary null controllability fails for some initial states in a heat equation with memory; the one-dimensional proof uses adjoint observability. | Different nonzero-initial-state question and equation; not a result about zero-start input kernel. |
| Halanay–Pandolfi (2015), original preprint arXiv:1404.2745 | Coleman–Gurtin memory; approximate controllability and lack of zero controllability for some initial states under stated hypotheses, using heat-equation results. The preprint explicitly discusses admissible endpoint evaluation of boundary-controlled states. | Different memory and reachability questions; no transfer to our modal theorem. Publisher metadata cross-checked, not all proof details independently audited. |
| Ivanov–Pandolfi (2009), DOI 10.1016/j.jmaa.2009.01.008 | Original proof inaccessible. Later primary work Guerrero–Imanuvilov describes a one-dimensional rest-controllability obstruction; secondary abstract describes Paley–Wiener interpolation. | Rest is stronger than temperature nulling; actual hypotheses and overlap remain unverified. Obtain full paper before claiming distinctness or novelty. |
