# Proof completeness report

The final draft expands the exact kernel proof into a positivity lemma, smooth biorthogonal right-inverse proposition, Hilbert–Schmidt tail lemma, Fredholm intersection proposition, endpoint lemma and theorem proof. It also derives the boundary sign, roots, spectral projectors, both terminal coefficient formulas, Laplace–Bessel identity and qualitative terminal-state claims. The square-root gap remains separate, with no change to the central theorem. The proof is conditional on CMV and Almira, plus standard Hahn–Banach, Riesz and Fredholm results. These standard results are cited to accessible notes rather than represented as new proofs.

The functional proof has been reconstructed but has not been independently certified. The PDE statement is conditional on the stated smooth-lift linear odd solution class; identification with the complete frozen physical model and any nonlinear preparation are unresolved. The comparison with Ivanov–Pandolfi remains incomplete because its original proof is inaccessible. No tests of finite truncations would certify the all-mode theorem, and none are offered as proof.
