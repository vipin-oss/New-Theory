# Candidate — NO-GO for now

## Finite thermoelastic periodic rod with a defect

**Problem:** in a finite A/B stack with a single thickness defect, determine transmission, spatial profile and resonance width for an existing MGT thermal law with a genuinely specified published two-phase Eringen elastic model. The local limit, already illustrated in `pilot/`, is the benchmark. Thermal and mechanical interfaces, the cross-interface kernel, exterior coupling, constitutive boundary conditions and power flux must be fixed consistently before nonlocal analysis. We have not specified or derived the nonlocal system, so there are no complete nonlocal governing equations to claim here.

**Proposed distinguishing test (not verified as new):** compare defect/no-defect and local/nonlocal cases with matched physical geometry, energy balance and finite-size behavior, including thermal relaxation sensitivity. Derive the nonlocal energy and interface conditions from the selected published model rather than assuming local transfer matrices still apply. Deliver transmission, localization, linewidth, mode/flux balance and convergence; validate against the elastic layered stack, local MGT and no-defect periodic responses. Risk: a mathematically well-posed heterogeneous model, admissible power accounting, and an unambiguous localized resonance may not exist for the selected choices.

**Three relevant, not verified closest, folder sources:**
1. Quintanilla (2019), *Moore–Gibson–Thompson thermoelasticity*, [10.1177/1081286519862007](https://doi.org/10.1177/1081286519862007), abstract and early/late passages read: local MGT foundation and coefficient-dependent stability; the inspected passages are not a finite nonlocal defect-stack result.
2. Eringen and Edelen (1972), *On nonlocal elasticity*, [10.1016/0020-7225(72)90039-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/0020-7225(72)90039-0), introduction read: nonlocal balance and constitutive theory; not a verified coupled MGT defect calculation.
3. Romano, Barretta, Diaco and Marotti de Sciarra (2017), *Constitutive boundary conditions and paradoxes in nonlocal elastic nanobeams*, [10.1016/j.ijmecsci.2016.10.036](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmecsci.2016.10.036), accepted-manuscript introductory and concluding passages: bounded-beam integral constitutive consistency, not the proposed thermoelastic finite stack.

**Outside-folder comparison:** Hosseini and Zhang, “Nonlocal coupled thermoelastic wave propagation band structures of nano-scale phononic crystal beams based on GN theory with energy dissipation: An analytical solution,” *Wave Motion* **92** (2020), 102429, [10.1016/j.wavemoti.2019.102429](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wavemoti.2019.102429), publisher abstract/introduction inspected in earlier application scan; full equations and defect content **unverified**. Jin, Hu and Hu, “Size effects on the mixed modes and defect modes for a nano-scale phononic crystal slab,” *Applied Mathematics and Mechanics* **44** (2023), 21–34, [10.1007/s10483-023-2945-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10483-023-2945-6), abstract inspected previously; full results **unverified**. Nonlocal thermoelastic periodic bands and elastic nonlocal defect modes are already known; an exact MGT/two-phase finite-defect priority claim is not established.

**Verdict:** NO-GO for now because the open gap is not verified, the nonlocal interface/energy method is not yet established, and the mandatory 152-paper and full-review analysis is unfinished. This is not a claim that the research is impossible. No additional candidates are recommended without those checks.
