## Detailed Specification of Candidate C1 (**#1 Ranked — CLEAR GO**)

### 1. Precise Problem Statement & Governing Equations
On a bounded thermoelastic domain $\Omega = [0, L]$ (or semi-infinite half-space $[0, \infty)$ as $L \to \infty$, or welded bimaterial $[ -L_1, 0 ] \cup [ 0, L_2 ]$), define the bi-exponential integral operator
$$(K_\xi \phi)(x,t) := \frac{1}{2\xi}\int_0^L \exp\!\left(-\frac{|x - x'|}{\xi}\right)\phi(x',t)\,dx', \qquad \xi > 0,$$
which is strictly self-adjoint and positive definite on $L^2(0,L)$ by Bochner's theorem ($\widehat{K_\xi}(k) = (1+\xi^2 k^2)^{-1} > 0$). Let $\gamma \in (0, 1]$ be Eringen's local elastic phase fraction.

**Theorem 1 (Variational Resolution of Form A vs. Form B — Closing Review Gap 8):**
In local linear thermoelasticity, completing the square in the Helmholtz free energy density gives the algebraic identity
$$\psi_{\mathrm{loc}}(\varepsilon, \theta) = \frac{1}{2}M_1\varepsilon^2 - \beta\theta\varepsilon - \frac{\rho c_\varepsilon}{2T_0}\theta^2 \equiv \frac{1}{2}M_1\left(\varepsilon - \frac{\beta}{M_1}\theta\right)^{\!2} - \frac{\rho c_\sigma}{2T_0}\theta^2, \qquad \rho c_\sigma = \rho c_\varepsilon(1 + \varepsilon_{\mathrm{cpl}}), \quad \varepsilon_{\mathrm{cpl}} = \frac{T_0\beta^2}{\rho c_\varepsilon M_1}.$$
Introducing two-phase spatial nonlocality $\mathcal{R}_\gamma := \gamma I + (1-\gamma)K_\xi$ into the two representations generates **two distinct, thermodynamically exact variational theories**:
1. **Variational Model V1 ($\mathcal{M}_\varepsilon$, Strain-Kernel Nonlocality $\implies$ Bounded Form B):**
   $$\Psi_{\mathrm{V1}}[\varepsilon,\theta] = \int_0^L \left[\frac{M_1}{2}\varepsilon\,\mathcal{R}_\gamma\varepsilon - \beta\theta\varepsilon - \frac{\rho c_\varepsilon}{2T_0}\theta^2\right]dx \implies \begin{cases} \sigma = \frac{\delta\Psi_{\mathrm{V1}}}{\delta\varepsilon} = \gamma M_1\varepsilon + (1-\gamma)w - \beta\theta, \quad w = K_\xi(M_1\varepsilon), \\ \rho T_0\dot{\eta} = -T_0\frac{d}{dt}\frac{\delta\Psi_{\mathrm{V1}}}{\delta\theta} = \rho c_\varepsilon\dot{\theta} + T_0\beta\dot{\varepsilon} = -\partial_x q. \end{cases}$$
2. **Variational Model V2 ($\mathcal{M}_{\varepsilon^{\mathrm{el}}}$, Elastic-Strain-Kernel Nonlocality $\implies$ Thermodynamically Repaired Form A):**
   $$\Psi_{\mathrm{V2}}[\varepsilon,\theta] = \int_0^L \left[\frac{M_1}{2}\left(\varepsilon - \frac{\beta}{M_1}\theta\right)\mathcal{R}_\gamma\left(\varepsilon - \frac{\beta}{M_1}\theta\right) - \frac{\rho c_\sigma}{2T_0}\theta^2\right]dx \implies \begin{cases} \sigma = \gamma(M_1\varepsilon - \beta\theta) + (1-\gamma)w, \quad w = K_\xi(M_1\varepsilon - \beta\theta), \\ \rho T_0\dot{\eta} = \rho c_\sigma\dot{\theta} + \frac{T_0\beta}{M_1}\dot{\sigma} = -\partial_x q. \end{cases}$$
3. **Reciprocity Violation of Literature's Standard Form A:** Pairing Form A stress ($\sigma = \mathcal{R}_\gamma(M_1\varepsilon - \beta\theta)$) with constant-strain local dilatation heating ($\rho c_\varepsilon\dot{\theta} + T_0\beta\dot{\varepsilon} = -\partial_x q$) violates $\delta^2\Psi/(\delta\varepsilon\delta\theta) = \delta^2\Psi/(\delta\theta\delta\varepsilon)$, injecting an indefinite internal power source $\frac{\beta}{M_1}\big[w\dot{\theta} + \theta(\dot{w} - \xi^2\partial_x^2\dot{w})\big]$ that destroys energy conservation even in conservative Green–Naghdi Type II thermoelasticity.

**Theorem 2 (Exact Bounded-Domain Differential Equivalence, Robin CBCs & Local Interpoint Availability Flux — Closing Q. R1, R2, R6):**
On $(0,L)$, $w = K_\xi\phi$ holds **if and only if** $(1 - \xi^2\partial_x^2)w = \phi$ on $(0,L)$ **together with the homogeneous Robin Constitutive Boundary Conditions (CBCs)**:
$$\partial_x w(0,t) - \frac{1}{\xi}w(0,t) = 0, \qquad \partial_x w(L,t) + \frac{1}{\xi}w(L,t) = 0.$$
Coupled with any passive member of the three-lag heat-flux template $q + \tau_q\dot{q} = -k(\theta_x + \tau_T\dot{\theta}_x) - k^*(v_x + \tau_v\dot{v}_x)$ ($\operatorname{Re} G(-i\omega) \ge 0 \iff k + k^*(\tau_v - \tau_q) \ge 0$ and $k\tau_T\tau_q \ge 0$), Model V1 obeys the **exact local interpoint availability balance**:
$$\frac{\partial \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{mech+th}}}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial P_x}{\partial x} = -\frac{1}{T_0}q\,\partial_x\theta, \qquad P_x(x,t) = -\sigma\dot{u} - \frac{(1-\gamma)\xi^2}{M_1}w\,\partial_x\dot{w} + \frac{\theta q}{T_0},$$
where integrating over $[0,L]$ and applying the Robin CBCs converts the boundary nonlocal flux jumps identically into the positive boundary-layer stored energy $\frac{(1-\gamma)\xi}{2M_1}[w(0,t)^2 + w(L,t)^2] \ge 0$. For $\gamma \in (0,1)$, the coupled dispersion relation is **cubic in $k^2$ (6 waves on $[0,L]$)**, adding an evanescent nonlocal boundary-layer mode $|\lambda_3| \sim (\sqrt{\gamma}\xi)^{-1}$ that singularly diverges as $\gamma \to 0^+$.

## Brief Specifications of Runner-Up Candidates (`C2`–`C4`)

### Candidate C2 (`GO — Runner-Up`): Analytical Passivity, Availability Functional & Front Classification for the 3-Lag Template
- **Governing system:** Linear anisotropic/isotropic Navier equations coupled to template (14): $q_i + \tau_q\dot{q}_i = -K_{ij}(\theta_{,j} + \tau_T\dot{\theta}_{,j}) - K^*_{ij}(v_{,j} + \tau_v\dot{v}_{,j})$, $\dot{v}=\theta$.
- **Key theorem:** Decomposing $q_i = -\frac{\tau_T}{\tau_q}K_{ij}\theta_{,j} + \tilde{q}_i$ yields an explicit quadratic 3D availability functional $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{3-lag}}$ with non-negative dissipation whenever $\tau_T\tau_q K_{ij} \succeq 0$ and $K_{ij} + (\tau_v - \tau_q)K^*_{ij} \succ 0$ (matching $\operatorname{Re} G(-i\omega) > 0$ proved symbolically in `pilot.py`), proving 3D contraction semigroup generation, 1D/radial exponential stability, and exact high-frequency front classification ($\tau_T > 0 \implies$ parabolic/diffusive high-$s$ smoothing of thermal front; $\tau_T = 0, \tau_q > 0 \implies$ hyperbolic double wavefronts) for all $\varepsilon > 0$.
- **Closest sources:** Quintanilla & Racke 2008 (Pos 52, uncoupled $\varepsilon=0$), Quintanilla 2019 (Pos 51, MGT $\tau_T=\tau_v=0$), El-Karamany & Ezzat 2016 (Pos 150, uniqueness only), Chiriţă & Zampoli 2024 (`10.1080/01495739.2023.2285791`, 4th-order rigid TPL).

### Candidate C3 (`CONDITIONAL GO`): Kapitza Interfacial Relaxation & Mode-Interference Decomposition in MGT/TPL Bimaterials
- **Governing system:** Two dissimilar MGT/TPL thermoelastic half-spaces separated by a non-isothermal imperfect interface $z=0$ with interfacial free energy $\psi_\Gamma([u], [v])$ and interfacial entropy generation $\Sigma_\Gamma = \frac{1}{T_0}q_n\big([\theta] + \tau_\Gamma[\dot{\theta}]\big) \ge 0$, separating bulk cross-mode complex-wavenumber interference $F_{\mathrm{int}}$ from strictly positive Kapitza dissipation $D_\Gamma$.
- **Closest sources:** `review paper.pdf` Sec. 6.10.4 (pp. 66–67), Sinha & Elsibai 1997 (Pos 48), Long & Fan 2022 (Pos 124), Jiang et al. 2024 (`10.1016/j.compstruct.2024.118675`), Sept 2026 GaAs/AlGaAs DPL heterojunction (`S0735193326022566`).

### Candidate C4 (`DEFER / PARTIAL`): Bounded-Domain Retarded Klein–Gordon Two-Phase Nonlocal Thermoelasticity
- **Governing system:** Replaces spatial kernel $K_\xi$ on $[0,L]$ with Lazar & Agiasofitou's (2022, Pos 9) causal retarded 1D Klein–Gordon Green's function $G_{\mathrm{KG}}(x-x', t-t') = \frac{c}{2\ell} H(t-t' - |x-x'|/c) J_0\!\big(\frac{c}{\ell}\sqrt{(t-t')^2 - (x-x')^2/c^2}\big)$, yielding frequency-dependent impedance CBCs $\partial_x\hat{w} \mp \sqrt{\ell^{-2} - \omega^2/c^2}\,\hat{w} = 0$ on $[0,L]$.
- **Reason for deferral:** Although mathematically novel on bounded domains, 2025–2026 saw rapid proliferation of unbounded differential KG thermoelasticity papers (Abouelregal et al. 2025, `10.1007/s00339-025-08329-w`; Sur et al. 2026; *J. Therm. Anal. Calorim.* May 2026, `10.1007/s10973-026-15539-8`), creating superficial keyword crowding compared to the cleaner, sharper gap of Candidate C1.
