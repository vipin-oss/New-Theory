# Gap map — partial, not verified open gaps

The review's taxonomy and research agenda are cited in `REPORT.md`; only its abstract, introduction and pp. 90–96 were examined. Review-stated gaps are hypotheses for screening, not evidence no one has solved them.

| Theme | Evidence actually examined | What the source does not establish / required check |
|---|---|---|
| Thermal family | Quintanilla (2019), initial and final passages: MGT introduced from GN-III, stability depends on coefficients; memory extensions discussed. Green–Lindsay (1972) and Green–Naghdi (1993) abstracts. | No verified 2020–26 classification of all admissible variants. Full exact-equation and original-assumptions comparison still required. |
| Nonlocal boundaries | Eringen–Edelen (1972) introduction and Romano et al. (2017) accepted-manuscript introductory/concluding passages distinguish whole-body balances, integral law and constitutive boundaries. | A beam example cannot prove all two-phase heterogeneous wave stacks ill-posed. Examine the exact selected kernel, limits, interface and energy flux; no proposed model is derived here. |
| Size effects and mechanical waves | Lim–Zhang–Reddy (2015) accepted-manuscript abstract: higher-order nonlocal strain-gradient elasticity, variational formulation and beam waves. | Not a complete survey of coupled thermal size effects or of defect modes; exact comparisons unread. |
| Defect localization | Prior local MGT pilot in this repository demonstrates a finite forced resonance in a chosen dimensionless stack. See `pilot/` and previous `paper/p4-pilot/RESULTS.md`. | A numerical resonance does not prove priority or an eigenmode, and it says nothing about a nonlocal heterogeneous kernel. The full set of folder papers and recent defect literature must be checked. |

Repeated limitations across **many** papers and contradictions are **not established**: too few primary papers have been inspected. In particular, a difference between a pure nonlocal integral beam and a local or two-phase model is not by itself a contradiction. No ingredient combination is claimed unique, and no evidence demonstrates a verified open gap yet.
