| **X1** | **Differential Eringen shortcut on bounded / semi-infinite domains vs. Integral Constitutive Boundary Conditions (CBCs)** | **Barak, Kumar, Kumar & Gupta 2023 (Pos 151)**, **Gupta et al. 2023 (Pos 152)**, **Zhao & Jin 2021 (Pos 97)**, **Tepe 2026 (Pos 111)** replace integral nonlocality on half-spaces/beams by $(1-\xi^2\nabla^2)\sigma_{ij} = \sigma_{ij}^L$ with classical BCs only. | **Romano, Barretta, Diaco & Marotti de Sciarra 2017 (Pos 47)** and **Jiashi Yang 2018 Ch. 7.5 (Pos 2)** prove that on any interval $[a,b]$ or half-line $[0,\infty)$, inverting the bi-exponential kernel requires **mandatory Robin CBCs** $w'(a)-\xi^{-1}w(a)=0$, $w'(b)+\xi^{-1}w(b)=0$; pure strain-driven Eringen ($\gamma=0$) is overdetermined/ill-posed under generic traction BCs. | **Resolved in Candidate C1 (`pilot/`):** Two-phase integral nonlocality ($\gamma\in(0,1)$) restores well-posedness by introducing a 3rd evanescent boundary-layer wave $|\lambda_3|\approx(\sqrt{\gamma}\xi)^{-1}$ governed by the Robin CBCs. Dropping CBCs causes a **22.3% failure** of the original integral equation near boundaries (`pilot/fig1_integral_residual.png`). |
| **X2** | **Nonlocal Thermoelasticity Form A (unsmoothed thermal stress) vs. Form B (smoothed thermal stress)** | **Barak et al. 2023 (Pos 151)**, **Gupta et al. 2023 (Pos 152)**, and `review paper.pdf` Eq. (54) write Form A $(1-\xi^2\partial_x^2)\sigma = M_1\varepsilon - \beta\theta$ alongside local dilatation heating $\rho c_\varepsilon\dot{\theta} + T_0\beta\dot{\varepsilon} = -\partial_x q$. | **Eringen 1974 (Pos 38, Eqs. 4.16–4.17 & 5.12)** derives both stress and entropy from a single nonlocal Helmholtz free energy $\Psi$, which forces nonlocal thermomechanical kernel $\beta'(|x-x'|)$ to appear **symmetrically** in stress AND dilatation heating; `review paper.pdf` Gap 8 (p. 93) flags Form A vs. Form B divergence near cutoff as unresolved. | **Resolved in Candidate C1 (`pilot/`):** Completing the square in $\Psi[\varepsilon,\theta]$ proves that Form B pairs with constant-strain entropy ($\mathcal{M}_\varepsilon$, Model V1), whereas Form A stress arises from nonlocal elastic-strain energy $\Psi_{\mathrm{V2}}[\varepsilon-\alpha_T\theta]$ and **must pair with constant-stress entropy** $\rho c_\sigma\dot{\theta} + (T_0\beta/M_1)\dot{\sigma} = -\partial_x q$ (Model V2). | **X4** | **Mode-Sum Energy Conservation ($\sum F_n = 1$) vs. Lossy Cross-Mode Interference at Interfaces** | **Sinha & Elsibai 1997 (Pos 48)** check $\sum F_n = 1$ in lossless/uncoupled limits; **Barak et al. 2023 (Pos 151)** and **Gupta et al. 2023 (Pos 152)** introduce a lumped "interaction energy" term alongside differential nonlocal stresses without interpoint nonlocal flux. | **`review paper.pdf` Sec. 6.10.3 Eq.
