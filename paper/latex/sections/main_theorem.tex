\begin{theorem}\label{thm:kernel}
Under \eqref{eq:2-1}, for every open interval \(I\) with \(\overline I\subset(0,T)\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:2-6}
\{k\in C_c^\infty(I):\mathsf Bk=0,\ (Qk)(T)=0\}
\end{equation}
is an infinite-dimensional real vector space. In particular \(\ker(\mathsf B|_{\mathcal K_{\rm adm}})\) is infinite-dimensional.

\end{theorem}

The proof uses \cite[Theorem~2.1]{Cannarsa2020} and \cite[Theorem~29]{Almira2007} as external inputs. It constructs solutions through exact operators and finite-dimensional algebraic choices; it does not provide decimal-valued closed-form controls.
