# Phase 1. Source map and priority limitations

Access date 2026-11-02. A DOI links to the original work, but except where stated only publisher abstracts or bibliographic metadata were available. Equations below are family-level schematic descriptions, **not quotations or verified full constitutive equivalences**. Symbols are local to each row. Original assumptions, boundary terms, theorem statements, parameters, and priority are unverified unless expressly stated. This is a targeted map, not an exhaustive systematic review.

**Strong overlap evidence.** Existing abstracts explicitly combine [Eringen and DPL](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11043-024-09669-5), [nonlocal elasticity and nonlocal nondissipative heat conduction](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00707-025-04407-5), [GN II and Eringen](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00707-025-04546-9), [nonlocal GL](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43452-025-01415-z), and [nonlocal MGT/GN](https://doi.org/10.1134/S0025654425607414). These publisher abstracts are not full-model comparisons. The remaining research question is whether this precise parallel-flux, mixed-local/nonlocal energy formulation or its estimates are genuinely distinct; no literature-supported priority gap has yet been established. Obtain full texts and compare governing equations, thermodynamic variables, admissibility conditions, geometries, and results before claiming novelty.
