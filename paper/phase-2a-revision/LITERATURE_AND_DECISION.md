# Phase 2a: source comparison and research decision

Evidence below is not an exhaustive novelty search. Primary sources were supplied by the user unless stated otherwise. Page/theorem checks are reported separately from a full line-by-line proof audit. In particular, both user-requested full papers are accessible and relevant proof sections were inspected, but **a complete independent line-by-line verification of all 25 pages of Fattorini–Russell or all 11 pages of Ivanov–Pandolfi is not claimed**. The original FR proof uses product estimates and embedding lemmas (§§3–4) and a Montel/norm-comparison argument (§5). Theorem 1.5(b) follows from the norm comparison and the adjoint of the restriction inverse, as on pp. 47–49. The zero/control distinction in Ivanov–Pandolfi is explicit in Definition 1, p. 2. Their paper also has positive scalar and wave examples, so describing the entire paper as containing only negative results would be inaccurate.

## Outcome, cost, and publication

The exact membership characterization in `sections/memory_trace.tex` is an **implicit coupled moment problem**, not an intrinsic sequence-space solution. Necessary entire-trace asymptotics exclude a concrete l² sequence; they do not decide the range closure or density. A norm lower bound is derived from the limiting trace in the boundary L² norm; it can vanish and is not a sharp inversion or exponential cost theorem. Fattorini–Russell's `exp(K sqrt(b_n))` concerns fast biorthogonals and cannot be converted into a cost for arbitrary prescribed memory traces without an additional solution theorem. Elementary compactness instability is not the headline.

The strongest existing kernel theorem remains dependent on external theorems and same-agent derivation, with no separate reviewer. Nothing here establishes a new result in priority/novelty terms. It would be premature to recommend any journal for submission. If independent review and a complete exact-range/novelty comparison establish substantial contribution, ESAIM: COCV is a *scope-appropriate venue to investigate*, not a current recommendation or a verified Q1 ranking; a negative editorial outcome is realistic on the present evidence. No claim of journal readiness is made.
