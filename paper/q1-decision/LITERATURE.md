# E5 — literature re-check and limits

A focused search, not an exhaustive literature survey; no chronological or novelty clearance. “Checked” means only the explicitly named portion was examined, not that the complete proof was independently verified. The user-provided archive is used locally, not republished.

1. **S. Ivanov and L. Pandolfi**, “Heat equation with memory: Lack of controllability to rest,” *J. Math. Anal. Appl.* **355** (2009), 1–11, DOI [10.1016/j.jmaa.2009.01.008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmaa.2009.01.008). Original PDF: Definition 1, equations (14)–(21), Obstructions 1–2, Lemma 1 and Theorems 2, 3, 5, 8 inspected. Uses an accumulating-root/entire-function mechanism for controllability to rest from arbitrary initial states of memory-on-Laplacian systems. The no-gap argument here uses related analyticity and is not proposed as novel. Equivalence of complete theorems **unverified**.
2. **H. O. Fattorini and D. L. Russell**, “Uniform bounds on biorthogonal functions for real exponentials with an application to the control theory of parabolic equations,” *Quart. Appl. Math.* **32** (1974), 45–69, DOI [10.1090/qam/510972](https://doi.org/10.1090/qam/510972). Original PDF: Thms 1.2 and 1.5(b) and §§3–5 proof strategy inspected. Fixed-time bound for separated fast exponentials, not an estimate for memory-target cost. Full proof audit **unverified**. Their “Exact controllability theorems for linear parabolic equations in one space dimension,” *Arch. Rational Mech. Anal.* **43** (1971), 272–292, DOI [10.1007/BF00250466](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00250466), supplied PDF Theorems 3.3–3.4 inspected; smooth compactly supported controls and parabolic moment methods are classical.
3. **S. Guerrero and O. Y. Imanuvilov**, “Remarks on non controllability of the heat equation with memory,” *ESAIM COCV* **19** (2013), 288–300, DOI [10.1051/cocv/2012013](https://doi.org/10.1051/cocv/2012013). Supplied published PDF, (1.1), (1.3), Theorems 1.1–1.2 checked: failure for some nonzero initial data; their second equation has memory on the state. Exact transformed-model equivalence and detailed proof comparison **unverified**.
4. **A. Halanay and L. Pandolfi**, “Approximate controllability and lack of controllability to zero of the heat equation with memory,” *J. Math. Anal. Appl.* **425** (2015), 194–211, DOI [10.1016/j.jmaa.2014.12.021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmaa.2014.12.021). Supplied accepted manuscript: equation (1.1), Theorems 1–3, admissibility of terminal boundary trace checked. They study temperature approximate controllability and failure of zero control for some initial data when their resolvent at T is nonzero. Published-version comparison and equivalence to E1–E4 **unverified**.
5. **F. Ammar Khodja, A. Benabdallah, M. González-Burgos and L. de Teresa**, “Minimal time for the null controllability of parabolic systems: The effect of the condensation index of complex sequences,” *J. Funct. Anal.* **267** (2014), 2077–2151, DOI [10.1016/j.jfa.2014.07.024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2014.07.024). Published PDF: (2.2), (2.10), Theorem 2.5 inspected. Its reciprocal-summability hypothesis fails for the combined spectrum with bounded accumulating slow branch; minimal-time conclusion does not directly transfer. Full 75-page proof comparison **unverified**.
6. **F. Ammar Khodja, F. Chouly and M. Duprez**, “Partial null controllability of parabolic linear systems,” *Math. Control Relat. Fields* **6** (2016), 185–216, DOI [10.3934/mcrf.2016001](https://doi.org/10.3934/mcrf.2016001). Publisher bibliographic page and [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/1701.05483), abstract, system (1.1), partial-null definition inspected: n parabolic equations, localized control, arbitrary initial data and projected terminal target. This is strong overlap in language and objective; it is not the same as an ODE memory component or zero-start output-kernel problem. Complete theorem-by-theorem comparison **unverified**.
7. **B. Shklyar**, “Partial null-controllability of evolution equations by one-dimensional control,” arXiv:2402.12855v1 (2024), [original preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.12855). Abstract, Definition 2 and assumptions (I)–(II) inspected. It assumes no finite spectral accumulation and a Riesz basis; the present slow branch has a finite accumulation. Full proof and final publication status **unverified**.
8. **G. Tenenbaum and M. Tucsnak**, “On the null-controllability of diffusion equations,” *ESAIM COCV* **17** (2011), 1088–1100, DOI [10.1051/cocv/2010035](https://doi.org/10.1051/cocv/2010035). Publisher PDF, Theorem 1.2 and spectral inequality (1.7) inspected; self-adjoint diagonalizable setting with eigenvalues tending to infinity. No direct transfer to combined accumulating spectrum; full proof audit **unverified**.
9. **P. Lissy and E. Zuazua**, “Internal Controllability for Parabolic Systems Involving Analytic Non-local Terms,” *Chinese Ann. Math. Ser. B* **39** (2018), 281–296, DOI [10.1007/s11401-018-1064-6](https://journal.hep.com.cn/caoms/EN/10.1007/s11401-018-1064-6). Publisher metadata/abstract only; author PDF retrieval failed. Analytic nonlocal couplings and compactness/uniqueness are overlapping methods, but system and targets differ. Full theorem and E1–E4 overlap **unverified**.
10. **S. Micu, C. Niță and L. E. Temereancă**, “Null-controllability properties of the heat equation with memory,” *Proceedings of the Romanian Academy, Series A* **22** (2021), 11–18. Search-indexed first page and theorem summary only; [publisher PDF](https://acad.ro/sectii2002/proceedings/doc2021-1/02-Micu.pdf) returned an anti-bot page on retrieval. It studies memory-type null controllability with moving control and an accumulating spectrum. Exact proof and overlap **unverified**; DOI not established.
11. **L. Pandolfi**, “The Heat Equation with Memory and Its Controllability,” in *Systems with Persistent Memory* (2021), DOI [10.1007/978-3-030-80281-3_3](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-80281-3_3). Publisher abstract and bibliographic metadata only: approximate controllability and null-target distinctions. Full chapter and equivalence **unverified**.
12. **G. Wang, Y. Zhang and E. Zuazua**, “Observability for heat equations with time-dependent analytic memory,” arXiv:2101.10615v3 (2023), [preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.10615). Abstract and equation (1.1) inspected: state memory, observability in space-time sets, partial-control discussion. Full theorem comparison and publication record **unverified**.

No searched item has been shown to settle the exact terminal-memory range of this frozen zero-start model; the restricted search and unread proofs do not establish that it is new. The numerical diagnostic is not a source of novelty evidence.
