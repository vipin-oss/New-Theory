## Is CMV needed instead of Fattorini–Russell?

**Not established.** In CMV arXiv v1, Remark 2.1 says their theorem completes/improves Fattorini–Russell's Theorem 1.5 and highlights explicit short-time/gap dependence. The current kernel construction fixes its parameters and only needs an `exp(C sqrt(b_n))` bound after time reversal; sharp dependence on both gap constants is not itself used. This makes a classical replacement plausible, but without reading Fattorini–Russell's exact theorem and checking lower/upper-gap and eigenvalue-asymptotic assumptions, it would be unjustified to assert the replacement for every `b_n` in the manuscript. The frozen abstract assumption `b_n asymp n^2` plus a lower square-root gap does **not** imply the precise expansion `b_n=r n^2+O(n)` or a uniform upper square-root gap. The physical sequence does have a stronger expansion; it is a separate case.

## Negative search result is not a novelty result

The literature overlaps substantially: exponential biorthogonals, Paley–Wiener interpolation, accumulating spectral branches, null controllability, and memory states have each been studied. None of the material actually inspected establishes an identical theorem; incomplete access means none rules one out either. A comparison must match (i) exact operator and sign, (ii) zero initial data and target, (iii) endpoint admissibility, (iv) single input vs full-state controllability, (v) regularity and (vi) actual versus closure of reachable range. Keep these distinctions in the next source audit.
