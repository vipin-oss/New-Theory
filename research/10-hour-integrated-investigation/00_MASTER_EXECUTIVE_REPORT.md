# Integrated investigation: interim executive decision

**Decision H: NO PUBLICATION-READY THEOREM YET.** This is an interim research package, not the completed ten-hour programme. Substantive model-specific linear theorems were derived, but novelty, independent expert review, the full nonlinear preparation question, and substantial requested computational/literature work remain open. The frozen post-zero experiment was not changed.

1. **Strongest results.** For the zero-start odd linearized PDE–ODE and boundary controls with a fixed positive terminal gap, the joint map to terminal temperature and memory has dense \(L^2_o\times L^2_o\) range. Nevertheless exact zero terminal temperature forces the control to vanish, and any sequence approximately reaching \((0,h)\), \(h\ne0\), must have unbounded \(L^2\) input norm. Also, without any gap, exact thermal nulling forces the odd input to vanish for smooth terminal-flat histories; the same holds for one-sign integrable histories. See 05–06.
2. **Novelty.** These are independently derived here; chronological novelty is **not verified**. The source comparison is selective and closely related negative memory-controllability work exists.
3. **Assumptions.** Constant reference coefficients, positive physical parameters with nonzero coupling, relaxed initial state, odd midpoint parity, appropriate smooth/terminal-gap controls, and linearization about equilibrium. Every statement has its own exact control class in 05–06. The active post-zero base is not replaced in the inverse experiment.
4. **Proof.** Moment and adjoint-pole arguments are complete at the stated modal/linear solution level, subject to the specified endpoint realization. They are not nonlinear physical preparation proofs.
5. **Audit.** A separate adversarial reconstruction by the same agent checked signs, convergence, residues, flatness and endpoint topology. This is not independent human verification.
6. **No-gap moment problem.** Solved negatively for terminal-flat smooth controls and for sign-definite integrable controls in the odd linearized setting; sign-changing nonflat smooth and general Sobolev controls remain open.
7. **Joint controls.** Their full linear system and formal adjoint were derived. Even mechanical/symmetric controls cannot cancel odd first-order temperature, but whether they achieve nonzero even or mean memory with exact thermal nulling is open.
8. **Nonlinear coupling.** A conditional mixed second-variation formula was derived; no feasible nonlinear branch or no-go theorem was proved.
9. **Physical compatibility.** Exact full thermal restoration with nonzero physically prepared memory remains open.
10. **Approximation.** Simultaneous \(L^2\) odd temperature/memory approximation is proved for the linearized fixed-gap model, with necessarily unbounded control cost for nonzero target; no nonlinear or \(H^2\) approximation is inferred.
11. **Reachable physical set.** Only the zero state is known to be physically reachable under exact thermal restoration.
12. **Finite-sine intersection.** Physical intersection remains open. In the odd linear flat class, nonzero finite-sine memory is impossible even without thermal nulling.
13. **Inverse transfer.** No physically reachable prior family or reachable-prior inverse theorem was established; higher-mode tails and frozen temperature topology remain obstacles.
14. **Numerics.** Synthetic modal calculations and independent algebra tests reproduce branch identities, mass inversion and severe finite-mode conditioning. They are not physical data or infinite-dimensional proof.
15. **Numerical artifacts.** Stable slow-root rationalization avoids a known cancellation; no high-precision, basis or quadrature convergence study was completed, so other artifacts were not ruled out.
16. **Counterexamples.** No exact counterexample to the stated theorems was found. A nonflat endpoint history defeats any attempt to extend the flat proof by ignoring its hypothesis. A nonzero flat function active arbitrarily close to the endpoint shows flatness alone is not vanishing.
17. **Failed/incomplete methods.** Standard separated moment biorthogonality fails its spectral-gap hypotheses; a nonlinear IFT requires an unproved right inverse; Lyapunov–Schmidt needs unproved closed range; neither failure proves impossibility.
18. **Literature.** Primary sources establish related memory-control obstructions, weighted transposition, scalar heat reachable spaces and finite-prior inversion, but none was shown to settle this exact coupled terminal assignment. See 13.
19. **Publication.** There is a potentially coherent linear control-theory contribution, but no publication-ready novelty or physical-preparation claim. `manuscript/` is explicitly a skeleton.
20. **Most important next problem.** Decide the sign-changing, nonflat no-gap infinite moment problem in an endpoint-admissible space; then address the full even joint system and nonlinear endpoint map.
