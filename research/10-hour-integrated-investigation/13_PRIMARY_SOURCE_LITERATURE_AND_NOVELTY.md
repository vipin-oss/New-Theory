# Primary literature comparison and limits

| Source and inspected portion | System and controls | Terminal constraint and result | Relation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Halanay–Pandolfi, *Approximate controllability and lack of controllability to zero of the heat equation with memory*, [arXiv:1404.2745](https://arxiv.org/html/1404.2745), Theorem 1, §§2–3 | Coleman–Gurtin, \(C^1\) kernel, boundary or interior | \(L^2\) boundary state may not have terminal value; admissible controls, temperature approximate reachability, non-null-control for some initial states | Our zero-start temperature-null/memory-target problem is different; establishes neither our theorem nor its novelty |
| Guerrero–Imanuvilov, *Remarks on non controllability of the heat equation with memory*, ESAIM COCV 19 (2013), 288–300, [publisher record](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.1051/cocv/2012013/), [full PDF](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1051/cocv/2012013.pdf), Theorems 1.1, 1.2, 2.1 and beginning of proof | Diffusion with integral memory and two boundary controls in 1D; paper also states a different zero-order memory case | Some nonzero initial temperatures cannot be driven to zero by any \(L^2\) boundary controls; proof uses an observability inequality and counterexample | Starting state, operator and question differ. Full proof of the analogous zero-order case is omitted in the paper; no comparison of its applicability is inferred |
| Allal–Hajjaj–Salhi–Sbai, [arXiv:2104.00610](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.00610), Theorem 1.1, (1.1), Definition 2.1 and Proposition 2.2 | Two degenerate/singular diffusive equations; one boundary control; Kalman condition | Transposition in weighted negative spaces, moment method; cited biorthogonal theorem requires uniform separation and summability | Slow spectrum here accumulates, violating hypotheses; direct transfer invalid |
| Boutaayamou–Et-tahri–Maniar, *Null controllability of an ODE-heat system with coupled boundary and internal terms*, [arXiv:2407.19515](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.19515), Theorem 1 and assumptions (2)–(3) | One scalar ODE coupled to boundary heat, Neumann control, bounded potentials and positive product of couplings | Null control from compatible \(H^1\times\mathbb R\) with \(H^{1/4}\) boundary input | Our internal variable is a field, not a scalar, and the target assigns memory rather than nulling it; cannot transfer |
| Avalos–Lasiecka, *Exact-Approximate Boundary Controllability of Thermoelastic Systems under Free Boundary Conditions*, [chapter PDF](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-0-387-35359-3_1.pdf), (1.1)–(1.3) | Kirchhoff plate, force/moment controls and Robin temperature, geometric hypothesis | Exact mechanical and approximate thermal steering | Different dynamics, geometry and target; proof not fully audited |
| Martin–Rosier–Rouchon, *On the reachable states for the boundary control of the heat equation*, [arXiv:1509.09072](https://arxiv.org/html/1509.09072), §1 | Scalar 1D heat, one/two boundary controls | Analytic reachability and Gevrey controls | Memory changes the operator and simultaneous constraint |
| Alberti–Santacesaria, *Infinite-dimensional inverse problems with finite measurements*, [arXiv:1906.10028](https://arxiv.org/html/1906.10028), §2.3 | Abstract finite-dimensional prior plus measurement projections | Finite measurements under balancing assumptions | Does not establish physical accessibility of the prior |

These are model- and theorem-level comparisons of selected primary sources, not an exhaustive search. The current flat-class argument could be a model-specific extension, but closely related memory-control obstruction literature exists. Chronological novelty and publication priority remain unverified. Earlier cited Ivanov–Pandolfi full original proof has not been inspected here. No journal recommendation or foundational-theory claim is warranted.
