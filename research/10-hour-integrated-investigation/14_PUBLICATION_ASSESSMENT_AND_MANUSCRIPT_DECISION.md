# Publication assessment

The flat no-gap theorem is a concrete analytic result extending the fixed-gap argument to controls active arbitrarily close to the terminal time. It is linearized, parity-restricted, and leaves nonflat control classes and nonlinear joint preparation open. It is mathematically nontrivial, but its independence from earlier Paley–Wiener and heat-memory obstruction literature is unverified. The full theorem's standard linear solution realization deserves outside referee scrutiny. Current evidence does not justify declaring a publication-ready novel paper or a solved physical preparation problem. A manuscript skeleton and explicit theorem-gap report are provided, not a disguised complete paper. The likely community is PDE control / inverse problems, not a new constitutive foundation. No journal recommendation is made.

The joint dense-range versus exact-temperature obstruction and necessary control-cost blowup adds a coherent model-specific contrast to the flat-class theorem. Its proof is an original derivation in this workspace, not yet certified chronologically new or independently reviewed. It may justify a later control-theory manuscript once priority, admissibility and expert review are complete; it still does not resolve the nonlinear physical experiment.
