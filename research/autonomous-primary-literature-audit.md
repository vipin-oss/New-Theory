## Explicit overlap assessment

Finite-dimensional Gram invertibility followed by a bounded perturbation estimate is elementary existing mathematics; the reference exponential-polynomial argument is a classical spectral-moment technique. The new finite-prior bound is a model-specific conditional application to a nonzero-load active base, not an automatically new general theorem. The inspected works show close substantive overlap and stronger conclusions in other settings; none of these inspected passages was shown to imply the exact active-base estimate with its constants. This is **not** evidence of publication-level novelty, and uncaptured prior art remains possible. A broader primary-source audit of finite-dimensional PDE–ODE initial-memory identification, and independent checking of constants and inherited estimates, are unfinished.
