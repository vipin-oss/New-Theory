# Candidate 3: inverse doping audit

**Question.** Does spatially resolved disjoint-contact voltage/current data identify an infinite-dimensional doping profile? This alternative stationary semiconductor problem is not frozen Direction 2. The equation and the exact mixed contacts are in `autonomous-five-candidate-discovery-audit.md` §3.

**CONDITIONALLY PROVED reduction, not the original mixed-contact theorem.** The primary paper [Leitão–Markowich–Zubelli, arXiv:2011.12200, (3) and Identification Problem 2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2011.12200) assumes unipolar transport, no recombination, unit mobility and voltage linearization at equilibrium. It obtains \(\operatorname{div}(\sigma\nabla u)=0\), \(\sigma=e^{V^0}>0\), and \(C=\sigma-\lambda_D^2\Delta\log\sigma\). Thus exact conductivity identification implies exact doping identification distributionally when the displayed relation holds. This is already the paper's reduction, not a new theorem.

With the additional assumptions that the *entire* exterior is Dirichlet, the boundary is connected, two admissible conductivities agree as a known smooth positive function on a common boundary collar, and their smooth elliptic problem enjoys boundary and interior unique continuation, equality of disjoint spatially resolved partial DtN bilinear forms implies equality of their full DtN maps. Proof: reproduce the two collar-UCP steps in `candidate-2-partial-data-shape-identifiability.md`; replace harmonicity by the common conductivity equation and use reciprocity. Consequently any applicable full-data isotropic conductivity uniqueness theorem transfers to this restricted class. This is a conditional implication, not a claim that the original paper's mixed-contact or arbitrary doping problem is solved.

**Hidden assumption.** Equality of doping in a collar does not imply equality of \(V^0\) or \(\sigma\) there: equilibrium solves a global semilinear elliptic problem. Thus the collar-conductivity assumption is substantive. Voltage-linearized data are an operator over all controls, not one integral measurement. Dropping a carrier, recombination and insulated boundary changes the physical model.

**OPEN:** disjoint contacts without known collar conductivity, the full bipolar nonlinear map, and relevant partial-data uniqueness. The 2006 paper's statement of an identification gap is historical, not proof it remains open. Compare its exact model with modern partial-data Calderón results before any claim. Related primary bibliography includes [Burger–Engl–Markowich, 2002](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4615-0113-8_3) and [Burger et al., arXiv:2011.11370](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.11370); the latter was checked only at abstract level this continuation. No numerical rank evidence or novelty claim.
