## Recalculations and limits

The reduction \(u_x=m+rp+\beta Qy\) and \(p_t=-\mu p+\gamma Qy\) gives exactly \((cI+d\theta Q)y_t-\kappa y_{xx}=-b\theta(m'+rp_t)+k\lambda w^2\). The fixed-point nonlinear residual is \(-dyQy_t-byE+k\lambda w^2\). Products use \(y\in L^\infty\) and \(y_t\in L^2\); no \(y_t\) supremum is needed. With \(R=2C_Bs\), the inherited constants imply \(\|\Phi y\|\le C_Bs+C_LC_N(s+R)^2\le R\) and \(\operatorname{Lip}\Phi\le1/4\). We did not independently validate each coefficient in \(C_L,C_N,C'_N\), so the classification is conditional rather than full independent verification.

For the linear odd problem, test by \(Y_t\) and \(P\); \(\|Y\|_\infty\le\sqrt L\|Y_x\|_2\) bounds \(BY\), and \(M\ge m_0\) gives a bound on \(\|Y_t\|_2\). The Gronwall coefficient \(1+\|B(t)\|_2^2+\|S(t)\|_\infty^2\) is integrable. The projected equation bounds \(Y_{xx}\) since its sine-Galerkin representative belongs to the same space. No \(M_t\) appears. This does not by itself substitute for construction and convergence of the Galerkin sequence.

For the positive-time upgrade set \(z_h=\Delta_hy\), \(\pi_h=\Delta_hp\), \(\Theta=\theta(t+h)\). The exact product is \(\Delta_h(\theta Qy_t)=\Theta Q(z_h)_t+z_hQy_t(t)\), hence the quotient equation has \(-dz_hQy_t(t)\) on the right. Test by \(z_h/\Theta\): the mass energy is \(c\int z_h^2/\Theta+d\|Qz_h\|^2\), retaining \(Q\), and diffusion produces \(\int |z_{h,x}|^2/\Theta-\int z_{h,x}z_h\Theta_x/\Theta^2\). The nonlinear products are bounded by Young with time-integrable factors \(\|y_t\|_2^{4/3}\) and \(\|\theta_x\|_2^4\). Averaging the quotient \(L^2\) norm on a fixed positive subinterval supplies a bounded starting energy; Gronwall gives uniform positive-time \(z_h\) bounds. Weak compactness then yields \(y_t\in L^\infty(\varepsilon,T;L^2)\cap L^2(\varepsilon,T;H^1_0)\). The omitted factor \(h\) in an explanatory undivided identity in the older report was already corrected in the independent audit; the quotient equation itself is correct. The argument does not give \(y_{tt}\), \(B_t\), or initial-corner regularity.
