# Cycle 10: adversarial audit and decision

**Decision: ASYMMETRIC PREPARATION REMAINS OPEN.** A terminal-temperature obstruction is proved strictly for the linearized odd dynamics and controls switched off on a positive terminal interval; it is not a universal nonlinear no-go theorem.

Audit of decisive links:

* Oddness removes \(Q\) only **after** linearization; the full nonlinear nonlocal mass and scalar mechanical control remain in the model. The odd boundary contributions add, not cancel. Mean and even dynamics are not conflated with odd memory.
* The slow eigenvalues accumulate at \(-\mu\); their fast partners diverge. The exact coefficients in the thermal terminal moment give the polynomial ratio \(b_n/\delta_n\). A positive time gap makes fast evaluations exponentially small; polynomial growth cannot defeat it. The accumulating points alone do not force an entire function to vanish unless the order-of-vanishing argument using the fast values is supplied, as done in the proof.
* Gap-specific result holds for \(L^2\) controls by the modal series extension, hence for smooth compactly supported controls. Controls touching \(s=0\) are explicitly excluded; no assertion about all endpoint-restored controls is justified. A nonlinear feasible curve may have a zero odd first derivative and a higher-order odd component, so no nonlinear exclusion is inferred.
* Unconstrained density uses the **full signed \(\ell^2\)** reference synthesis, not a finite matrix, positivity of individual modes, or numerical rank; its finite projection is onto. Exact finite-mode targets are nevertheless excluded under the gap, independently proved using both spectral branches and entire analyticity. \(L^2\) density is not \(H^2\) density, exact prior membership, or exact terminal thermal nulling.
* The constrained dual uses closure of \(\operatorname{ran}\mathcal A^*\), not a purported bounded separator. The infinite thermal condition is imposed mode by mode; zero boundary temperatures alone are insufficient. A possible nonlinear differentiability theorem and positivity are not assumed proved.
* Prescribed baths can be positive for small amplitudes, but this does not prove positive interior temperature, a physical nonlinear solution or a thermodynamically admissible nonzero terminal state. The entropy identity itself is not a controllability theorem.
* The frozen post-zero experiment and loading are unchanged. Projected control does not erase high-frequency tails; no transfer to the inverse theorem is established. The Cycle 9 symmetric obstruction remains settled, not reconsidered as open.

Independent human referee review, thorough literature priority search, coupled nonlinear well-posedness, no-gap moment analysis, and constrained nonlinear reachability remain outstanding. This is a same-agent self-audit, not scientific certification. Direction 1 remains rejected as a new foundational continuum theory; Direction 2 remains reformulate.
