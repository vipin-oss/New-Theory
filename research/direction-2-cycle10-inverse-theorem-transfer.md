# Cycle 10: transfer to the post-zero inverse theorem

No nontrivial nonlinear terminal-compatible prepared family \(\Psi\) is established. The linear unconstrained projected surjectivity is insufficient: resulting memory generally has a high-mode tail, the terminal temperature is not zero, and nonlinear odd/even coupling remains. With exact linearized temperature nulling and a switch-off gap, there are no nonzero odd tangent directions at all. Hence the Cycle 8 inverse theorem cannot yet be applied as a physical preparation theorem.

For a prospective \(H^2\)-valued family \(\Psi(a)\), first prove full initial temperature, mechanical equilibrium, positivity and terminal-control compatibility. Local injectivity requires a positive lower bound for \(D(\mathcal F\circ\Psi)(0)\) and a two-point derivative perturbation bound in a convex parameter set. A tail cannot simply be ignored: if \(D\Psi(0)v=v+E v\) for \(v\in V_N\), then under a **proved** extension of the actual observation derivative to the full directions used, a sufficient condition is \(\|\mathcal T_{\rm full}E\|<c_N\). Existing nonlinear \(H^2\)-based estimates do not automatically provide the needed full \(L^2\) tame bound. Alternatively compute a certified Gram of the actual prepared tangent response; neither route is established.

For the ideal symmetric protocol from Cycle 9 the odd family is impossible. Asymmetric baths remove that symmetry restriction, but the present unconstrained linear controllability and fixed-gap constrained obstruction do not establish a physical nonlinear family. The original post-zero measurement, material law and loading remain unchanged; no experimental performance is asserted.
