# Cycle 10: literature and novelty audit

- Halanay–Pandolfi, [arXiv:1404.2745](https://arxiv.org/html/1404.2745), Theorem 1, §§2–3: scalar Coleman–Gurtin memory, \(C^1\) kernel, boundary or interior control; approximate control of temperature and, under hypotheses, failure of null control. Their §2.1 modal Volterra representation and §3 density concern temperature state, not assignment of the coupled thermoviscoelastic internal memory while nulling temperature. They explicitly warn about endpoint evaluation for general \(L^2\) boundary controls. Our fixed gap avoids that issue; their result does not establish ours.
- Allal–Hajjaj–Salhi–Sbai, [arXiv:2104.00610](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.00610), (1.1)–(1.4), Definition 2.1 and Proposition 2.2: boundary transposition for two **diffusive** degenerate/singular equations, with matrix rank condition and weighted dual states. Their moment strategy is relevant but the present second equation has no diffusion and its slow spectrum accumulates; their theorem does not transfer. Our gap modal argument independently bounds the trace.
- Avalos–Lasiecka, [Exact-Approximate Boundary Controllability of Thermoelastic Systems](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-0-387-35359-3_1.pdf), equation (1.1), geometry (1.2) and steering (1.3): Kirchhoff plate on a two-dimensional domain, force/moment plus Robin thermal controls, exact mechanical target and approximate temperature; geometric and energy-space assumptions matter. This is not a one-dimensional quasi-static bar with internal ODE memory, nor exact temperature plus arbitrary memory. The accessible PDF was inspected through the problem statement; its complete proof was not independently checked.
- [arXiv:2512.13626](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.13626), §2.1: exponential kernels lift to parabolic–ODE, but the control is moving **interior** distributed input. The retrieved document ends after model setup/references, and promised theorem/proof sections are absent. No theorem is relied on or certified from its introductory claims.
- Martin–Rosier–Rouchon [arXiv:1509.09072](https://arxiv.org/html/1509.09072) and Orsoni [arXiv:1909.01644](https://arxiv.org/html/1909.01644): scalar boundary heat reachable spaces, not the coupled graph. Fabrizio–Giorgi–Pata [arXiv:0901.4063](https://arxiv.org/html/0901.4063), §1.1: minimal states versus histories, not physical controllability. The PDE–ODE Carleman system in [arXiv:2610.26157](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.26157) uses a different operator and interior/snapshot observations.

The fixed-gap terminal obstruction and exact finite-sine exclusion are independent deductions recorded in the accompanying reports, but the search is not exhaustive and no chronological novelty or priority is claimed. In particular, spectral accumulation, moment problems and analytic reachable-space obstructions are established themes. This is an Arena same-agent audit, not independent human verification. No foundational-theory claim or experiment is implied.
