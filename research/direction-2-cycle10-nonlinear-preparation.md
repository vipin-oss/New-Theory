# Cycle 10: nonlinear and thermodynamic limitations

Use the boundary lift of `direction-2-cycle10-asymmetric-preparation-model.md`. It converts inhomogeneous Dirichlet conditions into homogeneous traces plus explicit control-dependent terms in the **same** reduced PDE–ODE. Smooth control directions and strong-temperature solution spaces make the products \(yQy_s\), \(y(m'+rp_s)\) and \(w^2\) formally smooth; the prior zero-boundary contraction is suggestive of a Banach implicit-function proof for sufficiently small controls. The lifted forcing, constants, terminal map \((g_p,\tau_0,\tau_L)\mapsto(y(0),z(0))\), and uniform positivity must actually be estimated; this cycle does not supply a complete nonlinear controlled existence or \(C^1\) theorem. Boundary positivity alone does not imply interior positivity.

Even if such a theorem is established, the finite-dimensional submersion for \(\Pi_{V_N}P(0)\) applies only to the **unconstrained** map. It does not solve the infinite-dimensional equation \(y(0)=0\). With gap controls, the derivative of the odd terminal-temperature map has trivial kernel, so there are no nonzero odd first-order tangent directions satisfying exact thermal nulling in this control class. This does not exclude a nonlinear singular branch, nor controls with no switch-off gap. Also, thermal antisymmetry at first order need not produce a purely odd nonlinear state because quadratic terms create even components. Pure odd terminal memory requires an additional exact even/mean constraint.

For any sufficiently regular positive solution of the frozen equations, \(\eta=c\log(\theta/\theta_*)+b u_x\) satisfies
\[\eta_s-\partial_x(\kappa\theta_x/\theta)=\kappa\theta_x^2/\theta^2+k\lambda(u_x-z)^2/\theta\ge0.\]
This identity is inherited and rechecked in Cycle 9. Boundary reservoirs can exchange heat and entropy; it gives neither positivity nor terminal controllability. Mechanical equilibrium and memory evolution are enforced by the exact reduction; energy is finite under strong solution and positive bounded temperature assumptions. Zero controls supply a positive trivial solution with exact terminal restoration. There is no nontrivial positive nonlinear preparation, physical temperature range, stability of the terminal constraint, or experimental validation established here.
