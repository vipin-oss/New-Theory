# Cycle 6: scientific and foundational assessment

**Research-direction decision: REFORMULATE.** Treat Direction 2 as a model-specific, conditional boundary observation/state-identification question, not a proposed foundational continuum theory. A future valid graph injectivity theorem would be a model-specific inverse/observability theorem. Whether its proof is a substantive mathematical advance or a routine extension is **undetermined** until a complete proof and targeted primary comparison exist; a theorem for this PDE is not automatically a new constitutive principle. Direction 1 remains rejected as a new foundational theory.

The frozen model uses an internal variable with a relaxation ODE, stress coupling, heat balance, dissipation and a prescribed boundary loading. Solving \(z_t+\lambda z=\lambda\epsilon\) gives \(z(t)=e^{-\lambda(t-t_0)}z(t_0)+\lambda\int_{t_0}^te^{-\lambda(t-s)}\epsilon(s)ds\); thus an exponential hereditary kernel and internal-state representation are mathematically equivalent in this setting. This is not a new physical principle by itself. The full prehistory determines the initial state only under its own admissibility assumptions.

Primary comparisons inspected:

* Fabrizio–Giorgi–Pata, [arXiv:0901.4063](https://arxiv.org/html/0901.4063), §§1.1–1.2 and (1.1), explicitly discusses histories, distinct pasts with the same future, extended-state evolution and minimal states for viscoelastic convolution memory. Its abstract hyperbolic model and full future dynamics are not the present nonautonomous one-endpoint graph problem.
* Grasselli–Muñoz Rivera–Squassina, [arXiv:0806.0965](https://arxiv.org/html/0806.0965), §1 equations (1.1)–(1.2), studies a thermo-viscoelastic plate with hereditary mechanical and thermal kernels, explicitly assigned past history and boundary conditions. It establishes a different system and objective, not this graph theorem; it demonstrates that coupling temperature, mechanics and memory is established prior structure.
* [arXiv:2608.02117v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02117v2), §2.2–2.4, equations (5)–(11), uses an internal variable, free-energy-derived stress, a conjugate driving force and Clausius–Duhem inequality in finite viscoelasticity. This is a different tensorial, mechanical model, not a claim of exact equation equivalence, but confirms internal-variable thermodynamic structure is prior art.
* [arXiv:2606.18761](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.18761), §3 Theorems 3.1 and 3.7 as described in the inspected model/statement, analyzes a different heat-memory PDE–ODE operator with a finite spectral accumulation and analytic-semigroup framework. It is not this nonautonomous graph observation theorem.

Strongest defensible contribution **if the open problem is solved**: a precise theorem on recovery of an actual reachable initial memory from delayed boundary flux for this prescribed nonautonomous system, with hypotheses and limitations rigorously separated. Current proved results are the conditional bootstrap/endpoint estimates, comparison-model full injectivity and stability obstructions. No demonstrated novelty priority follows; primary search was targeted, not exhaustive. A foundational claim would require an independently specified new physical principle or constitutive law, thermodynamic and invariance analysis, an experimentally distinguishable quantitative prediction not reproduced by established models, and a credible primary-source comparison. None has been established here; no material data or proposed verified prediction are available.
