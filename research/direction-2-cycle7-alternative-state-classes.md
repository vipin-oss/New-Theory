# Cycle 7: admissible state classes and what they buy

| Class | Nonempty / closure | Meaning and identifiability |
|---|---|---|
| \(H_o=L^2_o\) | Infinite-dimensional closed Hilbert subspace. | Natural linearized state; actual observation is bounded. Kernel and adjoint density OPEN. No arbitrary endpoint trace. |
| \(X_o=H^2\cap H_o\) | Closed in \(H^2\), dense but not closed in \(L^2\). | Strong initial topology for the inherited nonlinear local map; odd endpoint values need not vanish. Regularity alone does not show injectivity; boundary strain adds at most its one continuous trace. |
| Coupled-past admissible initial states | The frozen base \(h=0\) is nonempty when its whole compatible past is supplied; the exact set, topology, closure and nonzero elements are not characterized. | The existing constitutive-ODE construction and endpoint compatibility do not establish mechanical equilibrium and heat evolution throughout the past. We do not identify this set with \(X_o\), a linear space, a ball or \(V_N\). Restricting a theorem to a subset gives conditional conclusions but does not prove it contains a practically useful neighborhood. |
| \(V_N=\operatorname{span}\{\sqrt{2/L}\sin(2\pi nx/L)\}_{1\le n\le N}\) | Finite-dimensional, closed in both norms, nonempty and contained in \(X_o\); finite sines vanish at endpoints. | Explicit parametric odd shape prior. For the fixed nonzero load under the proved symbolic threshold its derivative has a positive lower bound and the nonlinear map is locally two-point stable. It is a mathematical prior, not proof of physical preparation or coupled-past reachability. |
| Finite moments \(Mh=(\langle h,\psi_i\rangle)_{i=1}^k\) | Every \(\psi_i\in H_o\); fibers are closed affine sets and may contain infinitely many states. | No assertion that moments are identifiable for the actual \(\mathcal T\); exact and stable criteria are in the companion theorem. A finite prior is more restrictive than seeking finitely many moments of an otherwise unrestricted state. |

\(H^2\) is not \(H^2\cap H^1_0\): imposing a zero initial memory at endpoints would be an additional assumption, not a consequence of thermal Dirichlet values. Physical admissibility may be nonlinear and constrained by full history; no closure or density is inferred from a formal predicate. Nonlinear odd perturbations can generate even responses; parity formulas elsewhere are for the derivative about the even base, not a general nonlinear superposition.
