# Cycle 7: primary-literature and novelty audit

This is a targeted, non-exhaustive audit, not chronological novelty clearance. Sources below were inspected at the indicated portions, not inferred solely from abstracts.

* **Finite measurements and priors:** Alberti–Santacesaria, [Infinite-dimensional inverse problems with finite measurements](https://arxiv.org/html/1906.10028), Theorem 1 and §2.3 Hypothesis 1/Example 1: finite-dimensional compact convex prior, injectivity hypotheses on the map and derivative, and bounded finite-rank measurements approximating the identity. Their projection/measurement framework substantially overlaps the Cycle 7 finite-weight observation; our exact \(N\)-dimensional projection is a direct local consequence of the existing model-specific derivative bound. It is not a new general theory. The physical measurement and \(N\)-dependent Gram constant still require model-specific work.
* **Prior local transfer:** [Eller–Rieder](https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000137071/125311819), Appendix C Lemma C.1(a), was inspected in an earlier cycle; finite-dimensional injective derivative to local Lipschitz transfer is established methodology. The present attempt to re-fetch the PDF failed, so no new claim about its assumptions beyond that earlier audit is made.
* **Thermoviscoelasticity:** Grasselli–Muñoz Rivera–Squassina, [thermoviscoelastic plate with memory](https://arxiv.org/html/0806.0965), §1 equation (1.1) and boundary/history conditions: hereditary plate deflection and temperature on a bounded planar domain, not this quasistatic 1D active-base boundary inverse problem. It demonstrates prior coupled memory models, not our identifiability.
* **Memory state:** Fabrizio–Giorgi–Pata, [A new approach to equations with memory](https://arxiv.org/html/0901.4063), §1.1: indistinguishable histories and minimal-state concept for a class of convolution models. This motivates distinguishing physical past from state but gives no nonzero invisible state for our graph.
* **PDE–ODE observability:** [Coupled parabolic/hyperbolic–ODE Carleman](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.26157), proof (13)–(14) and Theorem 2.3: centered ODE estimate explicitly retains a weighted snapshot and uses interior observations, for another Kelvin–Voigt operator. No direct transfer to our single boundary observation or its graph. [Jha–George](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.22221), Assumptions 3.1 and 3.3: moving-control coverage and delay-compatible weight/cutoff; not the present fixed endpoint. These are methodological comparisons, not proofs of our adjoint density.
* **Moment/source inverse results:** [Kian–Sambou–Soccorsi](https://arxiv.org/html/1612.07942), Theorems 1.1–1.2, concerns a scalar separated source heat inverse problem; it does not establish identifiability of graph-linked initial ODE memory. General adjoint range versus closure criteria in the theorem report are standard Hilbert-space facts, not novel moment inversion.
* **Several experiments:** searches located Janno–von Wolfersdorf's [viscoelastic kernel paper](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/35245) and [thermal memory kernel paper](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/pdf/10.3846/1392-6292.2010.15.473-490), but both primary PDFs failed to fetch. Search snippets suggest different unknown kernels and measurement designs; without theorem text, their exact hypotheses and relationship remain **unverified**. No theorem claim is attributed to either. Alberti–Santacesaria's projection framework concerns multiple finite measurements, not the proposed same-memory repeated-loading protocol. A full primary-source comparison for repeated mechanical loading remains open.

## Classification

The original finite-prior lower bound and nonlinear local estimate are **previously documented conditional model-specific results**. Cycle 7's finite temporal-weight and noise corollary is a straightforward application; its prior-art overlap is substantial. The exact moment criteria, stacking identities and parity redundancies are standard or algebraic observations. Boundary strain is a model-derived rank-one observation, not proof that a new measurement achieves infinite-dimensional recovery. No genuine new foundational continuum theory and no demonstrated research novelty are established. Actual infinite-dimensional uniqueness remains open; the finite-prior proposal is promising only as an explicitly conditional applied inverse formulation.
