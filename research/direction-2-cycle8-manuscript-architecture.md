# Cycle 8: conditional manuscript architecture, not a submission-ready paper

**Proposed title:** Finite-Prior Recovery of Initial Internal Memory from Boundary Heat-Flux Measurements in a Thermoviscoelastic Bar

**Provisional abstract (conditional, for planning only):** We study a one-dimensional quasistatic thermoviscoelastic bar with a prescribed nonzero displacement history and fixed boundary temperature. Under an explicit small-loading regime, a previously derived active-base linearization leads to a coupled nonautonomous heat–internal-variable system. On any fixed finite odd sine prior, comparison with a constant-coefficient reference yields a positive lower bound for the linearized delayed boundary heat-flux observation; a local nonlinear two-point estimate follows from the smooth forward map. We also derive a conditional finite time-bin observation bound using the reference response and quantify sensitivity to bounded data error. The conclusions are restricted to the stated finite prior and depend on inherited well-posedness and regularity estimates. Physical preparation of the prior, quantitative parameter calibration, and recovery of arbitrary initial memory remain open. No experimental validation is claimed.

This is a feasibility outline requested for research planning, not a recommendation to submit or a fabricated results section. The abstract must be revised if any dependency is not validated or the preparation task fails.
