## A consistent dimensionless convention

Write \(\xi=x/L\), \(\tau=\lambda t\), \(U=u/L\), \(Z=z\), \(\Theta=\theta/\theta_*\), \(\widetilde\sigma=\sigma/(c\theta_*)\), and divide heat power by \(A_0\kappa\theta_*/L\). The input \(h=Z(\cdot,0)\), \(q=|g_*|/L\), and \(U_\xi\) are dimensionless strains; \(\tau\in(0,10)\), \(\xi\in(0,1)\). The equations retain the frozen form with
\[\widetilde a=a/(c\theta_*),\quad\widetilde k=k/(c\theta_*),\quad\widetilde b=b/c,\quad\widetilde c=\widetilde\theta_*=\widetilde L=\widetilde\lambda=1,\quad\widetilde\kappa=\kappa/(c\lambda L^2).\]
Indeed \(\widetilde c\Theta_\tau=\widetilde\kappa\Theta_{\xi\xi}-\widetilde b\Theta U_{\xi\tau}+\widetilde k(U_\xi-Z)^2\) and \(Z_\tau=U_\xi-Z\); stress has the same algebraic form. Use the transformed dimensionless flux factor \(2\widetilde\kappa\) when the output scale is \(A_0c\theta_*\lambda L\), or factor 2 under the heat-power scale stated above; these choices differ by \(\widetilde\kappa\). In either case **recompute every** \(C_L,C_B,s_*,C_{\rm tr},H_N,\sigma_N\) in the same normalization. Do not insert dimensional coefficients into mixed unweighted Sobolev sums or mix flux normalizations across constants. The symbolic threshold is then a dimensionless sufficient inequality, not an experimentally checked invariant numerical bound.
