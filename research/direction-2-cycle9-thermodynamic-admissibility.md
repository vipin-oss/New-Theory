# Cycle 9: thermodynamic and mathematical admissibility

For a sufficiently regular positive solution, set \(\varepsilon=u_x\), \(w=\varepsilon-z\), entropy density \(\eta=c\log(\theta/\theta_*)+b\varepsilon\), and heat flux \(q=-\kappa\theta_x\). The frozen heat equation yields
\[\eta_s-\partial_x(\kappa\theta_x/\theta)
=\kappa\theta_x^2/\theta^2+k\lambda w^2/\theta\ge0.\tag{1}\]
Indeed \(\partial_s\eta=c\theta_s/\theta+b\varepsilon_s\), so the reversible coupling cancels, leaving Fourier conduction and \(k\lambda w^2/\theta\). The boundary entropy flux need not vanish: a bath can remove heat and entropy. Nonnegative local production does not prove monotonicity of total specimen entropy, terminal impossibility, or temperature positivity.

A compatible free-energy density (up to reference/additive constants) is
\[\psi=\tfrac a2\varepsilon^2+\tfrac k2(\varepsilon-z)^2-b(\theta-\theta_*)\varepsilon+c[\theta-\theta_*-\theta\log(\theta/\theta_*)].\]
Then \(\psi_\varepsilon=\sigma\), \(-\psi_\theta=\eta\), and \(-\psi_z z_s=k\lambda w^2\ge0\). This is a consistency calculation for the frozen equations, not a new constitutive principle. Under the selected strong/positive solution assumptions on a finite interval, \(y\in C_sH^1\), \(z\in C_sL^2\), \(u_x\in C_sL^2\), bounded \(\theta\) and \(1/\theta\) give finite mechanical/thermal energy and integrable entropy-production terms. Derive (1) first for smooth fields; passing to the stated weak-initial class requires standard chain-rule/approximation justification and is not independently redone here.

**Verified positive preparation:** the zero control produces \(u=z=0,\theta=\theta_*\), everywhere positive, zero entropy production, finite energy, exact terminal constraints and full equilibrium. This trivial solution does not supply nonzero memory. For any nonzero admissible control, positivity, existence, finite energy and uniqueness are part of the **selected solution class**, not concluded from the entropy identity. For sufficiently small unconstrained controls, the fixed-point framework suggests an open well-posed class and continuous dependence, but arbitrary-control constants and the persistence of the exact terminal-temperature constraint are not proved here. In this zero-memory-start strong class, the exact Volterra formula and \(y\in L^2_sH^2_x\) give \(p\in H^1_sH^2_x\), so terminal \(z\in H^2\) and is even. This does not establish reachability or higher regularity. Small changes in the actuation preserve the symmetry obstruction inside the unique-solution class, but generally may violate the exact terminal condition.
