## Scientific assessment

The potentially differentiated point is the rigorously controlled delayed endpoint trace generated by **initial nondiffusive memory in the actual nonautonomous thermoviscoelastic base**, with no frozen equilibrium replacement and no additional endpoint derivative of \(S\). Combining this with a realizable small-load active base and the nonlinear full-output obstruction is a coherent theorem package. It may merit expert examination, but the existing evidence shows an application of familiar correction, energy, density, compactness and implicit-function tools; it does not yet show a conceptual advance beyond the closely related literature. Neither an approximate invisible sequence nor a singular-value upper bound resolves exact identifiability. No empirical values establish that the sufficient parameter bound describes the intended experiment.
