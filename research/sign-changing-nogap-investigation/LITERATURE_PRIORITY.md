# Primary-source comparison and priority

| Primary source, inspected point | Precise issue and assumptions | Relation to paired moments |
|---|---|---|
| Guerrero–Imanuvilov, [Remarks on non controllability of the heat equation with memory](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1051/cocv/2012013.pdf), ESAIM COCV 19 (2013) 288–300, Thms. 1.1, 1.2, 2.1 and §2 | Certain nonzero initial states cannot be driven to zero under two \(L^2\) boundary controls; one branch of the adjoint spectrum tends to \(-1\), the other to \(-\infty\); proof constructs finite combinations violating observability | Strongly related spectral accumulation, but different initial/terminal question. Its text states the analogous zero-order memory theorem without giving that proof. It does not immediately settle zero-start control injectivity |
| Halanay–Pandolfi, [arXiv:1404.2745](https://arxiv.org/html/1404.2745), Thm. 1, §3 | Coleman–Gurtin diffusive memory, \(C^1\) kernel, \(L^2\) boundary controls; endpoint evaluation may require special admissibility | Establishes a crucial endpoint-space warning, not the present paired interpolation identity |
| Allal–Hajjaj–Salhi–Sbai, [arXiv:2104.00610](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.00610), Thm. 1.1, condition (1.6), Definition 2.1 | Two diffusive equations; moment biorthogonality requires a separated spectral sequence and summability | Slow branch has a finite accumulation point, so those hypotheses fail; applying the theorem to the paired spectrum would be invalid |
| Martin–Rosier–Rouchon, [arXiv:1509.09072](https://arxiv.org/html/1509.09072), §1 | Classical scalar heat boundary reachable states, analytic spatial ranges and Gevrey control construction | No nondiffusive ODE field or temperature–memory paired moments |
| Boutaayamou–Et-tahri–Maniar, [arXiv:2407.19515](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.19515), Thm. 1 | Heat equation coupled to a scalar ODE at the boundary; compatible \(H^1\times\mathbb R\) data, \(H^{1/4}\) Neumann control | Different finite-dimensional ODE, different spectrum and null target; cannot transfer |
| Ivanov–Pandolfi, *Heat equation with memory: Lack of controllability to rest*, J. Math. Anal. Appl. 355 (2009), 1–11, bibliographic record at [Numdam](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.1051/cocv/2012013/) | Primary full paper not retrieved; publisher/research listings describe Paley–Wiener interpolation for rest control | Original theorem hypotheses and proof not checked; substantial overlap is possible and priority remains unknown |

This search is limited. The analytic-control argument combines elementary jet, entire-extension, and Laplace-tail ideas; whether it is known in an equivalent formulation is unresolved. No novelty, priority, or publication readiness is claimed.
