# Theorem-gap statement; no manuscript submitted

The strongest complete new derivations in this workspace are analytic-class no-gap uniqueness, the exact jet hierarchy and its non-sufficiency, and the conditional infinite-tail and bounded-control statements. They are candidates for a later paper, not yet independently reviewed or checked for priority. No proof resolves the required smooth nonanalytic sign-changing interpolation. In particular, the infinite-dimensional family in §10 of the master report satisfies the jet constraints only: its discrete moments may be nonzero. The missing step is either construction of a nonzero member of the kernel of the actual paired moment operator, with all endpoint and terminal-state conditions, or a proof that this kernel is trivial. Standard moment biorthogonality does not apply directly because of slow spectral accumulation. An adequately reviewed solution, careful comparison to Ivanov–Pandolfi and related primary results, a complete terminal-space treatment, and independent verification are required before publication. No finished LaTeX manuscript is claimed.
