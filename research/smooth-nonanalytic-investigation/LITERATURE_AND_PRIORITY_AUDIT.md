# Literature and priority audit

* Almira, [Müntz Type Theorems I](https://arxiv.org/html/0710.3570), Theorem 29: for distinct real \(\lambda_k\), power span is dense on \([a,b]\), \(0<a<b\), iff \(\sum_{\lambda_k\ne0}|\lambda_k|^{-1}=\infty\). The actual fast \(b_n\asymp n^2\) have finite reciprocal sum; the variable change \(x=e^{-t}\) yields fast-only incompleteness on every subinterval. The theorem concerns an unperturbed power family, not the coupled tail operator. This is an expository primary-accessible theorem/proof, not a priority claim for the original theorem.
* Guerrero–Imanuvilov, [2013 article PDF](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1051/cocv/2012013.pdf), Theorems 1.1–1.2 and §2: constructs an observability counterexample for specific heat-memory systems and some initial data; the 1D proof has slow eigenvalues accumulating at \(-1\). It does not establish the present zero-start boundary-input injectivity.
* Halanay–Pandolfi, [arXiv:1404.2745](https://arxiv.org/html/1404.2745), Theorem 1: scalar diffusive-memory control; arbitrary \(L^2\) boundary input may not admit strong terminal evaluation. Its admissibility warning is directly relevant, its operator and target differ.
* Wang–Zhang–Zuazua, [arXiv:2101.10615](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.10615), §1, study analytic memory kernels and distributed observability. Analyticity of the **kernel** is not the same as quasianalyticity of a boundary control; no theorem is transferred.
* Ivanov–Pandolfi, *Heat equation with memory: Lack of controllability to rest*, J. Math. Anal. Appl. 355 (2009), 1–11, [bibliographic record](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.1051/cocv/2012013/): the actual original paper and proof were not accessible during this attempt. The bibliographic link is not used as a theorem source. In particular its exact Paley–Wiener hypotheses and comparison remain unverified. Search records point to closely related rest-controllability work, so priority cannot be claimed.

This is a focused nonexhaustive search; no independent human proof or literature review was performed. No publication-ready novelty statement is made.
