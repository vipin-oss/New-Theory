# Reproducible numerical investigation (not a theorem)

`code/smooth-nonanalytic/study.py` computes the Bessel Nyström inverse for three different flat-correction families. Synthetic dimensionless values are \(T=L=1,\ M=2,\ \gamma=.8,\ S=.35,\ \kappa=1,\ \mu=.7\), \(d=.14\) and \(B=-\gamma S/\mu\); these are test inputs, not a material fit. Coefficients use stable quadratic-root formulas. For n=5,10,20,40,80 the number of basis functions is 2n+8. The fit enforces n scaled paired residuals, the endpoint v(T), and first-memory coefficient =1; it then tests ten withheld moments. The Gram matrix is the actual physical-control \(L^2\) Gram, and columns are whitened only after discarding eigenvalues below \(10^{-13}\) of the largest. The reported Gram condition number is **only for retained directions**; least-squares rank uses relative cutoff \(10^{-12}\). Singular values include rows of differing physical scale and are not intrinsic observability constants.

All data: `diagnostic.csv`, `diagnostic-80.csv`, `diagnostic-80-q800.csv`. At n=80 and Q=400, polynomial/oscillatory/compact families have ranks 19/12/6, control norms 297/305/354, first derivatives -0.422/-0.422/-0.422, finite first-90-mode memory norms 1.316/1.316/1.316, and withheld residuals about \(1.5\times10^{-11}\). First memory =1 merely fixes a scale, not a target for all memory modes. Endpoint residuals are around \(10^{-14}\)–\(10^{-13}\). Their computed full-design condition ratios are around \(10^{16}\)–\(10^{17}\), close to/beyond double precision.

The Q-versus-2Q discrepancy at n=80 is approximately \(2.0\times10^{-11}\) in paired residuals for polynomial and oscillatory families; for compact bumps it is \(7.2\times10^{-5}\) in paired residuals and \(1.8\times10^{-3}\) in memory. At Q=800 it improves to \(5.2\times10^{-9}\) and \(2.9\times10^{-6}\) for bumps, still above their nominal fit. There is a quadrature floor at high mode numbers as Gauss nodes miss fast boundary layers. Thus nominal withheld residuals may be quadrature artifacts, especially for bumps, and should not be compared with exact zero. Parameters, basis error, truncation, Nyström and integration error, SVD/Gram truncation, roundoff, and withheld error are distinct; Q/2Q disagreement is an empirical indicator, not a certified bound.

`test_operator.py` independently checks at 60 decimal digits the tail formula using incomplete-gamma series and polynomial moments versus a one-dimensional Laplace integral, including a positive nonzero tail. It is not an arbitrary-precision solve at n=80. The earlier separate 60-digit diagnostic tested one n=5 sine fit. Neither gives high-precision convergence of these three families. No precompactness, uniform exact constraints, or limiting nonzero control is proved; bounded-looking finite norms do not give a construction. Additional high-precision, independently converged sampling and a genuine compactness/limit argument remain required.
